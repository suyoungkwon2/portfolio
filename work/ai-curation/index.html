AI Curation:
Scaling Themed Campaigns

“You name the theme. AI does the rest.”

At Kurly, every themed campaign on the homepage was picked by hand. When the team behind them shrank, the homepage went stale. I led an AI system that turns a theme into a full campaign and keeps it fresh on its own.

01 CONTEXT

Curation Is What Kurly Sells

Kurly built its brand on picks: customers trust that if Kurly shows a product, it’s worth buying.

On the homepage, themed campaigns bring a new theme and a fresh mix of products every day. It’s where customers discover something new and enjoy shopping.

But the Campaigns Went Stale

A space meant to show something new and good every day started showing the same campaigns and products, day after day.

With fewer merchandisers (MDs) on the curation team, keeping the campaigns fresh kept getting pushed back.

kurly.com

02 IMPACT

From Hand-Picked to Self-Running

Implemented an AI system that curates as well as Kurly’s expert MDs. In an A/B test, its campaigns matched their hand-picked ones on clicks and purchases. With curation handed to the AI, MDs could focus on the work that needs their judgment most.

03 ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITY

Turning an MD’s Craft into a System

  1. Strategy

    Set the plan from the interviews: give MDs an internal copilot first, then test with customers, then launch. Framed it as step one of homepage personalization.

  2. UX

    Interviewed 8 MDs and marketers to map the workflow. Designed the Campaign Copilot UI and the Google Sheet MDs launch campaigns from, and trained MDs on every update.

  3. Execution

    Ran the internal evaluations and the A/B test, connected AI curation to Kurly’s inventory and content management systems, and led QA through launch.

  4. Alignment

    Kept the CEO and the MD, marketing, and data teams aligned from model choice to rollout.

04 USER PROBLEM

The Homepage Stopped Surprising Anyone

Kurly customers love the products its MDs pick for them, and “the joy of discovery” is one of the company’s goals.

But as campaigns went untouched, the homepage lost its variety: the same campaigns ran for weeks on end, and with no one managing the products, more and more of them were sold out or no longer sold.

As a result, customers were losing the joy of finding new products.

08 INTERNAL COPILOT

A Copilot That Cuts Curation Time

I designed and delivered Campaign Copilot, an internal tool that supports how MDs plan campaigns. Since only employees would use it, a custom front end wasn’t worth the time, so I chose Gradio to ship it fast and clean. It had two goals:

  1. 1. Cut the time spent finding and picking products. It reads what a theme is after, finds products that fit, and shows what MDs need to choose them in one view.
  2. 2. Raise curation quality until MDs are satisfied with it. Every search they ran and rated showed us where the AI fell short.
  • Open to every employee
  • 6 updates in 8 weeks from MD feedback
  • Reads product descriptions, not just names

10 A/B TEST

Could Customers Tell the Difference?

To decide whether AI could run the homepage, I designed a 2-week A/B test around one question: would customers notice who did the picking?

Objective
Validate AI curation quality before the final integration decision
Hypothesis
AI-curated campaigns perform as well as MD-curated ones
Method
Same homepage slot, same theme; only who picked the products changed
Target
24,278 users in A (MD) vs 24,455 in B (AI)
Conditions
20 slots, at least 30 products per campaign, a new theme every day
Duration
2 weeks
Promotional BannersLimited-Time OffersBest SellersSpecial CampaignsThemed Campaigns20 test slots

Where it ran

The test ran in the 20 themed-campaign slots at the bottom of Kurly’s homepage. Both groups saw the same slots and the same themes.
Only the products changed: group A got the MD’s picks, group B got the AI’s.

A new theme every day.

11 INTEGRATION

Name a Theme, Get a Live Campaign

Going live had two sides:
a launch MDs could run on their own,
and the rules that keep every campaign full and fresh once it’s live.

1 · Launch from the sheet MDs already use

What This Project Taught Me

  1. 01

    Defining the Right Bar

    The goal was not to beat our MDs but to match them. Setting parity as success made a sensitive change, letting AI pick for a curation brand, something the company could say yes to.

  2. 02

    Trust Before Scale

    Putting AI in MDs’ hands first turned the people with the most at stake into the people shaping it. Their feedback made the model better, and their trust made the rollout possible.

  3. 03

    One Step in a Bigger Plan

    Framing this as Project 1 of a personalization roadmap kept every decision pointed somewhere. The Sheet, the hourly refresh, and the data it collects all set up what comes next.
