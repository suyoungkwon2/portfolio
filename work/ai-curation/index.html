AI Curation: Scaling Themed Campaigns

AI-driven scaling of themed campaigns

Period
Jun – Dec 2024
Role
PMA/B Test DesignUX ResearchCEO Communication
Team
1 ML engineer1 backend engineer
Org
Kurly
10s Summary

What?

Automated themed campaigns with AI, now running 200+ campaigns across 25% of Kurly's key homepage slots, with no human curation.

Why?

With fewer merchandisers, campaigns went stale, sold-out items lingered, and there was no way to scale.

How?

Built a system where you type a theme and AI picks, ranks, and refreshes products every hour. An A/B test showed it performs on par with human curators.
