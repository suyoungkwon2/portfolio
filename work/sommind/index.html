SomMind: Insomnia Digital Therapeutic

Digital Therapeutic App

Period
June 2022- March 2023
Role
Team LeadPMUX DesignClinical Program & Trial DesignRegulatory
Team

Org
Asleep × Seoul National University Bundang Hospital
10s Summary

What?

Built a 4-week insomnia therapy app with Seoul National University Bundang Hospital, so a clinic-only treatment can be done at home. It earned medical-device certification and K-FDA approval for its clinical trial.

Why?

The best drug-free treatment for insomnia means weekly clinic visits, paper sleep diaries, and too few trained therapists, so most patients end up relying on sleeping pills instead.

How?

Broke the therapy into short animated lessons and a quick daily sleep diary, and had the app adjust each patient's bedtime every week based on how they actually slept.
