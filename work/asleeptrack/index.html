AsleepTrack: B2B Sleep AI Platform

10s Summary

What?

Turned Asleep's sleep-tracking AI into an API, SDK, and dashboard that other companies plug into their own apps. It became the company's first B2B revenue line, at $60K+ MRR.

Why?

Asleep's AI tracked sleep more accurately than Apple Watch or Fitbit, but plugging the raw model into a client's product was so hard that no one could adopt it at scale.

How?

Wrapped the model in a simple module that needs only a phone microphone, with docs and pay-as-you-go pricing. SK Telecom, LG, and KB Healthcare signed on within 3 months of launch.
